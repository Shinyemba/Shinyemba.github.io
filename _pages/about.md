I am a Lecturer in the Department of Computing, Mathematical and Statistical Sciences at the University of Namibia (UNAM), where I teach statistics, demography and research methods. I completed a PhD in Geography (Spatial Statistics) at Birkbeck, University of London, in 2026. My thesis, supervised by Dr Shino Shiode (Birkbeck) and Prof. Karen Devries (London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine), modelled the geography of violence against children and young people in Namibia using the Violence Against Children and Youth Survey.

I also hold an MSc in Applied Statistics and Demography, a BSc (Hons) in Population Studies with Statistics, and a Diploma in Applied Statistics from UNAM, and a Postgraduate Diploma in Public Health (Distinction) from the University of Pretoria. Before joining academia, I worked as Monitoring and Evaluation Manager at the Namibia Planned Parenthood Association (NAPPA). I am a Teaching Assistant in the School of Health Systems and Public Health at the University of Pretoria, and a research collaborator on the MiFood and Women Feeding Cities projects. I do most of my analysis in R.

Research interests
======
* Spatial analysis with application to population health
* Modelling demographic data using indirect estimation methods
* Analysis and evaluation of fertility and mortality data
* Food security, child nutrition, and population health
* Adolescent sexual and reproductive health
* Monitoring and evaluation
